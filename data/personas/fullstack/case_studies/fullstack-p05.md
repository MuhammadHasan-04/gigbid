# Real-time collaboration feature for fintech

**Client:** Harbor Supply (fintech) · **Year:** 2023

## Problem
Harbor Supply needed help with real-time collaboration feature for fintech. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using WebSockets, Node.js, hooks-based frontend and a key-value store. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project shipped in 9 weeks.
