# Checkout and subscriptions for a real estate site

**Client:** Harbor Supply (real estate) · **Year:** 2022

## Problem
Harbor Supply needed help with checkout and subscriptions for a real estate site. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Stripe, React, server-side JS, OAuth and PostgreSQL. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project saved the team 5 hours per week.
