# Marketing site and CMS for a SaaS brand

**Client:** Cedar Health (SaaS) · **Year:** 2025

## Problem
Cedar Health needed help with marketing site and cms for a saas brand. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and preview deployments. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project shipped in 6 weeks.
