# Marketing site and CMS for a insurance brand

**Client:** Northwind (insurance) · **Year:** 2021

## Problem
Northwind needed help with marketing site and cms for a insurance brand. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using server-rendered React, component-based SPA, Tailwind CSS, Vercel and TypeScript. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project reduced cloud cost by 15%.
