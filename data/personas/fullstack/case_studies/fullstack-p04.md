# Django platform for e-commerce bookings

**Client:** Greenroots NGO (e-commerce) · **Year:** 2023

## Problem
Greenroots NGO needed help with django platform for e-commerce bookings. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Django, PostgreSQL, Python, card payments, a key-value store and a background task queue. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project saved the team 18 hours per week.
