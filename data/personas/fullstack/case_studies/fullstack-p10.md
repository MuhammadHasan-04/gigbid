# Cross-platform mobile app for non-profit

**Client:** Tidal Pay (non-profit) · **Year:** 2022

## Problem
Tidal Pay needed help with cross-platform mobile app for non-profit. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using React Native, TypeScript and Node.js. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project shipped in 5 weeks.
