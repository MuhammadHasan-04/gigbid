# Django platform for edtech bookings

**Client:** Harbor Supply (edtech) · **Year:** 2026

## Problem
Harbor Supply needed help with django platform for edtech bookings. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Django, a relational database, pythonic tooling and a background task queue. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project reduced cloud cost by 39%.
