# Marketing site and CMS for a edtech brand

**Client:** Orbit Freight (edtech) · **Year:** 2022

## Problem
Orbit Freight needed help with marketing site and cms for a edtech brand. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using the app router, hooks-based frontend, typed JavaScript and edge deployment. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project saved the team 12 hours per week.
