# Shopify store customisation for healthcare

**Client:** Harbor Supply (healthcare) · **Year:** 2021

## Problem
Harbor Supply needed help with shopify store customisation for healthcare. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Liquid theme work, JavaScript and React. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project improved accuracy from 73% to 85%.
