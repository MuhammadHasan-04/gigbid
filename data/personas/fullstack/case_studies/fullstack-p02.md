# Marketing site and CMS for a e-commerce brand

**Client:** Shieldline (e-commerce) · **Year:** 2021

## Problem
Shieldline needed help with marketing site and cms for a e-commerce brand. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using the app router, React, edge deployment and Tailwind CSS. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project reduced cloud cost by 41%.
