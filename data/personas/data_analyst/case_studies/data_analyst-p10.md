# Survey data analysis in R for a insurance study

**Client:** Bluefin Labs (insurance) · **Year:** 2025

## Problem
Bluefin Labs needed help with survey data analysis in r for a insurance study. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using R, confidence intervals, data cleaning and Excel. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project cut manual review time by 42%.
