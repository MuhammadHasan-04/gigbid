# GA4 tracking audit for a logistics website

**Client:** Orbit Freight (logistics) · **Year:** 2021

## Problem
Orbit Freight needed help with ga4 tracking audit for a logistics website. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Google Analytics 4 and serverless SQL on GCP. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project cut manual review time by 54%.
