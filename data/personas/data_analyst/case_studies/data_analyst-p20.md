# BigQuery warehouse and reporting for manufacturing

**Client:** Shieldline (manufacturing) · **Year:** 2023

## Problem
Shieldline needed help with bigquery warehouse and reporting for manufacturing. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using BigQuery, window functions, data pipelines, Google Analytics 4 and Looker. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project reduced cloud cost by 48%.
