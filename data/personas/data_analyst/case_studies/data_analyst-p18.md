# A/B test analysis for fintech growth team

**Client:** Stackwise (fintech) · **Year:** 2024

## Problem
Stackwise needed help with a/b test analysis for fintech growth team. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using split tests, confidence intervals, tidyverse and window functions. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project saved the team 15 hours per week.
