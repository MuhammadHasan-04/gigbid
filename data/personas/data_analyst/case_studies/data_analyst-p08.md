# Excel financial model clean-up for media

**Client:** Orbit Freight (media) · **Year:** 2020

## Problem
Orbit Freight needed help with excel financial model clean-up for media. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using spreadsheets, data cleaning, Google Sheets and Power BI. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project shipped in 8 weeks.
