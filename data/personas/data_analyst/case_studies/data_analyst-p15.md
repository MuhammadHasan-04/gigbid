# Executive KPI dashboard in Power BI for healthcare

**Client:** Greenroots NGO (healthcare) · **Year:** 2024

## Problem
Greenroots NGO needed help with executive kpi dashboard in power bi for healthcare. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Power BI, SQL, dashboard design and Excel. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project improved accuracy from 76% to 81%.
