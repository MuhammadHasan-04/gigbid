# GA4 tracking audit for a insurance website

**Client:** Lumen Realty (insurance) · **Year:** 2022

## Problem
Lumen Realty needed help with ga4 tracking audit for a insurance website. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using web analytics events, serverless SQL on GCP and executive dashboards. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project saved the team 7 hours per week.
