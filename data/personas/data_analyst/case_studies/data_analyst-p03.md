# dbt models on Snowflake for real estate

**Client:** Stackwise (real estate) · **Year:** 2023

## Problem
Stackwise needed help with dbt models on snowflake for real estate. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using modular data models, SQL, warehouse credits, ETL, Looker and automated ingestion. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project shipped in 5 weeks.
