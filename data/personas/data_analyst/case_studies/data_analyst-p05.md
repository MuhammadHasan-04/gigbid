# Retention and cohort analysis for a non-profit app

**Client:** Northwind (non-profit) · **Year:** 2020

## Problem
Northwind needed help with retention and cohort analysis for a non-profit app. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using cohort analysis, complex queries, Python, confidence intervals, dataframes and Metabase. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project brought latency down to 80 ms p95.
