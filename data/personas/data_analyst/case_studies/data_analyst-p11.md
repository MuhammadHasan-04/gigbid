# BigQuery warehouse and reporting for real estate

**Client:** Kitebook (real estate) · **Year:** 2025

## Problem
Kitebook needed help with bigquery warehouse and reporting for real estate. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Google's warehouse, complex queries, data pipelines and GA4. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project reduced cloud cost by 38%.
