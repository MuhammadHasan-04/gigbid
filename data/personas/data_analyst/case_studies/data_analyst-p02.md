# Tableau sales dashboards for a healthcare company

**Client:** Orbit Freight (healthcare) · **Year:** 2021

## Problem
Orbit Freight needed help with tableau sales dashboards for a healthcare company. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Tableau workbooks, SQL, dashboard design, data cleaning and a cloud data warehouse. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project shipped in 4 weeks.
