# BigQuery warehouse and reporting for travel

**Client:** Pixelpress (travel) · **Year:** 2023

## Problem
Pixelpress needed help with bigquery warehouse and reporting for travel. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using BigQuery, complex queries, extract-transform-load jobs, GA4, governed BI layer and dbt. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project brought latency down to 120 ms p95.
