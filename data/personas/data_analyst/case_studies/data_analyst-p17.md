# Executive KPI dashboard in Power BI for logistics

**Client:** Cedar Health (logistics) · **Year:** 2020

## Problem
Cedar Health needed help with executive kpi dashboard in power bi for logistics. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Power BI, complex queries, executive dashboards and Excel. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
