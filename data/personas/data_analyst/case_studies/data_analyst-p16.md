# Survey data analysis in R for a non-profit study

**Client:** Lumen Realty (non-profit) · **Year:** 2026

## Problem
Lumen Realty needed help with survey data analysis in r for a non-profit study. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using tidyverse, hypothesis testing and pivot tables. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project cut manual review time by 17%.
