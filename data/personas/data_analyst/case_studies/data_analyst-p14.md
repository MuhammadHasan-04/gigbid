# Excel financial model clean-up for healthcare

**Client:** Forge Metals (healthcare) · **Year:** 2021

## Problem
Forge Metals needed help with excel financial model clean-up for healthcare. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using pivot tables, deduplicating records and Power BI. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project improved accuracy from 78% to 82%.
