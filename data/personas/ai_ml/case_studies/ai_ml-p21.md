# Defect detection with computer vision for manufacturing

**Client:** Cedar Health (manufacturing) · **Year:** 2020

## Problem
Cedar Health needed help with defect detection with computer vision for manufacturing. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using python 3, computer vision, YOLO, ONNX and OpenCV. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project raised conversion by 22%.
