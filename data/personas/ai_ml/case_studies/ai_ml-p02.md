# Multi-agent workflow for real estate operations

**Client:** Cedar Health (real estate) · **Year:** 2021

## Problem
Cedar Health needed help with multi-agent workflow for real estate operations. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using pythonic tooling, LangGraph, GPT function calling and prompt engineering. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project reduced cloud cost by 30%.
