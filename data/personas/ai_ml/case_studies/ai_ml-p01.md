# RAG chatbot over logistics documents

**Client:** Stackwise (logistics) · **Year:** 2020

## Problem
Stackwise needed help with rag chatbot over logistics documents. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Python, retrieval-augmented generation, LangChain, FAISS and pgvector. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project served 38k users in the first month.
