# Churn / risk prediction model for a media company

**Client:** Stackwise (media) · **Year:** 2021

## Problem
Stackwise needed help with churn / risk prediction model for a media company. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Python, classical ML pipelines, XGBoost, experiment tracking, containerised services and pandas. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project brought latency down to 180 ms p95.
