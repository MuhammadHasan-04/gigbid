# Fine-tune an open LLM for real estate support tickets

**Client:** Orbit Freight (real estate) · **Year:** 2023

## Problem
Orbit Freight needed help with fine-tune an open llm for real estate support tickets. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Python, instruction tuning an open model, Hugging Face Transformers, PyTorch and SageMaker endpoints. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project raised conversion by 50%.
