# Demand forecasting for logistics

**Client:** Stackwise (logistics) · **Year:** 2025

## Problem
Stackwise needed help with demand forecasting for logistics. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using python 3, time series forecasting, boosted tree ensembles, dataframes and MLflow. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project raised conversion by 19%.
