# Productionise ML models for a e-commerce team

**Client:** Tidal Pay (e-commerce) · **Year:** 2020

## Problem
Tidal Pay needed help with productionise ml models for a e-commerce team. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using model deployment pipelines, Docker, MLflow and AWS SageMaker. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project improved accuracy from 83% to 89%.
