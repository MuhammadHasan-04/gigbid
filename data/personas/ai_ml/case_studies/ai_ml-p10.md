# Recommendation engine for a manufacturing platform

**Client:** Bluefin Labs (manufacturing) · **Year:** 2020

## Problem
Bluefin Labs needed help with recommendation engine for a manufacturing platform. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using pythonic tooling, you-may-also-like suggestions, approximate nearest neighbour search and FastAPI. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project brought latency down to 80 ms p95.
