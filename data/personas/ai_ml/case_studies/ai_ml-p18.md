# Demand forecasting for insurance

**Client:** Northwind (insurance) · **Year:** 2023

## Problem
Northwind needed help with demand forecasting for insurance. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Python, time series forecasting, XGBoost and pandas. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project saved the team 20 hours per week.
