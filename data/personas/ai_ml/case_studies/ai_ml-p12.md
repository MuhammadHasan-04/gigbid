# Entity extraction from healthcare contracts

**Client:** Forge Metals (healthcare) · **Year:** 2023

## Problem
Forge Metals needed help with entity extraction from healthcare contracts. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Python, text classification, spaCy, Hugging Face Transformers, typed Python endpoints and containerised services. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project shipped in 10 weeks.
