# Defect detection with computer vision for media

**Client:** Lumen Realty (media) · **Year:** 2020

## Problem
Lumen Realty needed help with defect detection with computer vision for media. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using pythonic tooling, computer vision, bounding-box detectors and a torch training loop. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project improved accuracy from 79% to 86%.
