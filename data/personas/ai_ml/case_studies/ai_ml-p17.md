# Churn / risk prediction model for a e-commerce company

**Client:** Quill Learning (e-commerce) · **Year:** 2024

## Problem
Quill Learning needed help with churn / risk prediction model for a e-commerce company. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using pythonic tooling, scikit-learn, XGBoost, MLflow and FastAPI. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project cut manual review time by 46%.
