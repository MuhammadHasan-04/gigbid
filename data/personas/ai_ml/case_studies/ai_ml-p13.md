# Productionise ML models for a fintech team

**Client:** Stackwise (fintech) · **Year:** 2026

## Problem
Stackwise needed help with productionise ml models for a fintech team. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using keeping models healthy in production, Docker, experiment tracking, Airflow and Celery. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project brought latency down to 250 ms p95.
