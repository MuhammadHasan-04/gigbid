# Churn / risk prediction model for a insurance company

**Client:** Tidal Pay (insurance) · **Year:** 2024

## Problem
Tidal Pay needed help with churn / risk prediction model for a insurance company. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using pythonic tooling, scikit-learn, boosted tree ensembles and Docker. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project cut manual review time by 47%.
