# Defect detection with computer vision for healthcare

**Client:** Bluefin Labs (healthcare) · **Year:** 2026

## Problem
Bluefin Labs needed help with defect detection with computer vision for healthcare. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using python 3, computer vision, YOLO, OpenCV, optimised inference graphs and a torch training loop. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project raised conversion by 43%.
