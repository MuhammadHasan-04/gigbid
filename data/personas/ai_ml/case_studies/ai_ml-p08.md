# Entity extraction from logistics contracts

**Client:** Pixelpress (logistics) · **Year:** 2020

## Problem
Pixelpress needed help with entity extraction from logistics contracts. Their team had tried spreadsheets and ad-hoc scripts, and the process did not scale.

## What I did
I designed and built the solution end to end using Python, NLP, spaCy, typed Python endpoints, Hugging Face Transformers and container images. I ran weekly demos and wrote handover docs.

## Result
The project saved the team 17 hours per week.
